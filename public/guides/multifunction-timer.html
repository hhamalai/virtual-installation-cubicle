How to wire a multifunction time relay (timer)

A multifunction time relay delays, extends, pulses or repeats a switching action. One device covers many jobs — a staircase light that turns itself off, a pump that keeps running for a while after a signal stops, or a warning light that flashes. This guide explains the terminals, shows the wiring, and describes every timing function, and you can build it all in the free browser simulator.

The terminals

  • A1 / A2 — supply (line to A1, neutral to A2). This powers the device.
  • B1 — control input. A switch/button between line and B1 is the control signal used by some functions.
  • 15 — output common (COM) of the changeover contact.
  • 16 — output normally-closed (NC): connected to 15 while the output is OFF.
  • 18 — output normally-open (NO): connected to 15 while the output is ON.

Basic wiring

  1. Wire line L to A1 and neutral N to A2 to power the timer.
  2. Bring a line to the output common 15.
  3. Take the load from 18 (on when the output is active) or 16 (on when the output is idle); return the load to N.
  4. For control-based functions, wire a switch or button from line to B1.
  5. Tap the timer's front face to open settings: pick a function and set the time (0.1–60 s).

The green U/t LED shows the supply is present; the yellow R LED shows the output contact is active (15–18 closed).

Functions

"t" is the time you set. The output is the changeover contact (15 common, 18 NO, 16 NC). "Supply permanent" means A1/A2 stay powered and the function is driven by the control input B1.

CodeNameBehaviour
EOn-delayWhen the supply is applied, the output switches ON after time t and stays on until the supply is removed.
EcOn-delay with controlSupply permanent. Closing B1 starts t; the output switches ON after t and is held while B1 stays closed.
ROff-delay with controlSupply permanent. Closing B1 switches the output ON immediately. When B1 opens, the output stays ON for t, then switches OFF.
WuInterval (on power-up)When the supply is applied, the output switches ON immediately for t, then OFF — and stays off until the supply is removed and reapplied.
WsImpulse (control close)Supply permanent. Closing B1 gives one ON pulse of length t. A further pulse needs B1 to reopen and close again.
WaImpulse (control open)Supply permanent. Opening B1 gives one ON pulse of length t.
BpFlasher (pause first)When the supply is applied, the output flashes evenly (t off, t on, repeating) until the supply is removed.

Worked example: staircase light that turns itself off

  1. Choose function Wu (interval) and a time of, say, 10 s.
  2. Wire the supply: L → A1, N → A2.
  3. Wire the lamp through the output: L → 15, 18 → lamp L, lamp N → N.
  4. Feed the supply through a push button so power is applied on press: the lamp comes on and switches off automatically after 10 s.

Swap the function to E for an on-delay, or Bp to make a lamp flash. For a run-on (pump keeps going after a stop signal) use R with a control switch on B1.
